CubeRunner
===

*Blocks are falling over your head! Can you outrun them?*

> **This fork ports CubeRunner 2.5.2 (Bukkit/Spigot 1.8-1.12) to Paper 26.3.**
> Build it with `./gradlew build`. See [PORTING.md](PORTING.md) for what changed, the requirements, and what still
> needs testing in game.
